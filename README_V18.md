# NHMEC Herbalist Learning App — Version 18

Version 18 corrects **NHMEC 202 Kitchen PENothecary™** so it is no longer presented as a course under construction.

## Version 18 change
Kitchen PENothecary™ now follows the current seven-section PENothecary™ course structure:
1. Welcome & Foundations
2. The 6 Foundational Herbs
3. Herbal Preparations (Hands-On)
4. Building Your Wellness Plan
5. Family Herbal Care
6. Lifestyle Integration
7. Advancing Your Training

NHMEC 201 Kitchen Herbalist remains a separate textbook-based course.

Existing Kitchen PENothecary™ videos, Canva presentation, Google Docs, and Google Forms are reused. Where the supplied source materials do not identify the exact Doc/Form-to-section mapping, Version 18 keeps those links in clearly labeled Assignment Resources and Submission Forms rather than guessing.
